This Cyble Research & Intelligence Labs (CRIL) weekly vulnerability report covers two views of risk: what attackers tried against Cyble's global honeypot sensors, and what the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) disclosed for industrial control systems (ICS). The Cyble Global Sensor Intelligence (CGSI) network observed exploitation attempts against about 20 known vulnerabilities. They range from newly disclosed flaws in WordPress, Apache Solr, and Langflow to years-old bugs in GPON home routers and embedded TCP/IP stacks.
