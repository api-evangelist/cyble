Cyble has rolled out a significant upgrade to Executive Monitoring inside Cyble Vision, bringing unified findings, AI-driven scoring, and expanded alerting together in a single protection suite. Executive monitoring has historically meant stitching together several things at once. An impersonation tool here, a dark web exposure feed there, a reputation score from somewhere else, and alerts that show up in whatever channel each vendor happened to support.
