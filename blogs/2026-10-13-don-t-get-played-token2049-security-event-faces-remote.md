A crypto security event that promised to teach attendees how to avoid scams is now the subject of scam allegations. The gathering, branded "Don't Get Played," was held as a side event during TOKEN2049 week in Singapore and co-hosted by groups operating under the names "Apex Accelerator" and "Stone Venture." According to a police report filed by one attendee with the Singapore Police Force, the organizers allegedly pressured guests to install software that would have handed the organizers remote control of their computers.
