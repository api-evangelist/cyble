Every Cybersecurity Awareness Month campaign rests on four habits: Strong passwords, Multifactor authentication, Spotting phishing, and keeping software updated. All four assume a person is the one signing in, reading the message and clicking install. At a growing number of companies that person is now an AI agent , and AI agent security has no equivalent of the annual training.
